Green Vancouver: Tree Species Visualization with Altair

🧭 Overview

Welcome to Green Vancouver — an interactive data-driven journey through Vancouver’s neighborhoods to explore the biodiversity of its urban forest. Using a 5,000-row dataset of street trees from the City of Vancouver, this project leverages Altair to uncover patterns in tree species by geography, street side, and neighborhood diversity.

Inspired by my six years of hiking and nature-watching in Vancouver, this analysis combines personal curiosity with civic data to bring green spaces to life.

❓ Research Questions

This project answers four key questions:

🌳 Neighborhood Ecology
What types of trees are planted across different Vancouver neighborhoods?
📊 Species Count
What is the total count and variety of tree species planted citywide?
🗺️ Geospatial Distribution
How are tree species distributed by latitude and longitude?
↔️ Street Side vs Genus
Are certain species or genera more likely to appear on even vs odd street sides?

🌱 Why This Matters

Urban forestry impacts everything from air quality and biodiversity to aesthetics and public health. By visualizing Vancouver’s tree data, we can:

Support urban planners in tree placement
Inform ecologists about biodiversity distribution
Inspire citizens to appreciate and engage with their local environment
